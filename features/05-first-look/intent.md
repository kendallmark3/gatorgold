# Feature 5: First look

Progressive Intent v1, feature 5. Parent intent: [intent.md](../../intent.md).

## Intent

Make Gator Gold something you want to play the moment you see it. Give the game a real logo, and make the screen feel alive and inviting before the first spin.

## Inputs

- Mark's request after playing feature 4: "put the logo in there some way" and "make the interface look more appealing, you'd want to play it as soon as you see it."
- The approved look from the first mockup: swamp green and gold, the gold cabinet, the gator mascot, the big red SPIN button.
- No logo artwork was supplied, so the logo is designed here.

## Outputs

- A Gator Gold logo: an emblem and the name, used at the top of the game and as the browser tab icon.
- A screen that moves while it waits: the logo, the lights, the mascot, and the SPIN button each draw the eye.
- A machine that shows what there is to win before the player spins.
- A page that looks finished on a laptop as well as a phone.

## Success criteria

1. The logo is on screen at all times and reads as "Gator Gold" at a glance.
2. The browser tab shows the logo's emblem.
3. Before any spin, the screen is visibly in motion without being touched.
4. The SPIN button is the most eye-catching control on the screen.
5. The top prize at the current wager is shown on the machine and changes with the wager.
6. The mascot invites the player to spin when the game has been idle.
7. Everything still fits one phone screen with nothing cut off, and nothing overlaps.
8. Play is unchanged: the same spins pay the same amounts, and sound still works.
9. With the device set to reduce motion, the screen holds still and remains playable.

## Constraints

- Keep the approved colours and layout. This is polish, not a redesign.
- No change to odds, payouts, or spin timing.
- No images or files to download beyond the fonts already used.
- No copying another company's logo or artwork.

## Evidence

- Screenshots of the game at rest on a phone and on a laptop, and during a win.
- A check that the screen is moving before the first spin and holds still under reduced motion.
- A rerun of the play checks from feature 3 to show play is unchanged.
- Mark's reaction. Whether it makes you want to play is not something a check can decide.

## Stop

Stop when each criterion has evidence. More polish waits for Mark's reaction.
