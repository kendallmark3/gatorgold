# Feature 7: Free spins

Progressive Intent v1, feature 7, the last for now. Parent intent: [intent.md](../../intent.md).

## Intent

Give the player something to play toward. When they have earned it, the game hands them free spins, plays those spins itself, and lets them win. Finish filling the screen: the gators at the sides should move as if in the water, and the emptier right side should have something on it.

## Inputs

- Mark's request after feature 6: "I want progressive"; "when you decide that the player has earned it, give them five free rolls or whatever you think and then spin the wheel yourself"; "let them win"; like a Las Vegas bonus game.
- Also from Mark: make the gators in the water move a little; "the right side of the screen doesn't have anything, so put something on the screen"; and it must still look good on a laptop and on a phone.
- The machine, sound, scenery, and screen sizing from features 1 to 6.

## Outputs

- A meter on the machine that the player can watch fill.
- Free spins awarded when the meter is full, played by the game without the player pressing anything, with a running total shown at the end.
- A retuned payout table, so the game as a whole still returns what it did.
- Side gators that move as if floating, and more on the right side and across the top of a wide screen.

## Success criteria

1. A meter on the machine shows progress toward free spins and how far there is to go, on every screen size.
2. Each paid spin moves the meter by the same amount, so the player can see they are earning it.
3. A full meter awards five free spins, announced clearly.
4. The game plays the free spins itself. The player cannot spin or change the wager until they finish.
5. Free spins cost nothing, and every free spin pays something.
6. The total won from the free spins is shown at the end and matches what was added to the balance.
7. Free spins pay at the wager the player was using while filling the meter, and changing the wager cannot be used to fill the meter cheaply and cash it in dearly.
8. Long-run return, free spins included, is between 94% and 98%.
9. The side gators visibly move as if in water, and the right side and top of a wide screen are no longer bare.
10. Everything still fits and scales on the six screen sizes from feature 6.
11. With the device set to reduce motion, the new scenery holds still.

## Constraints

- The free-spin rule is stated to the player, in the payout view. It is not a hidden adjustment.
- Paid spins stay independent, honest draws. The game does not look at the balance to decide any result, and never steers a player toward a loss.
- Nothing the player needs lives only in the scenery, because phones do not show it.
- No bonus wheel, pick-a-prize screen, or growing jackpot in this feature.

## Evidence

- Automated checks of the meter, the free spins, and the accounting.
- The exact return with and without free spins, checked against a long simulated session.
- The running game filmed in stills: the meter nearly full, the award, a free spin winning, the total, and the game afterwards.
- Screenshots at the six screen sizes.
- Mark's reaction, and then Stephanie's.

## Stop

Stop when each criterion has evidence. This is the last feature before Stephanie plays; what comes next is decided by her reaction.
