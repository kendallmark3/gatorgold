# Feature 6: Fill the screen

Progressive Intent v1, feature 6. Parent intent: [intent.md](../../intent.md).

## Intent

Make Gator Gold fill whatever screen it is on. On a laptop or tablet the machine sat in a narrow column with empty dark space around it. Give it surroundings, make the name read cleanly, and scale it properly on a phone, a tablet, and a laptop.

## Inputs

- Mark's reaction to feature 5: the game itself "looks good, it's perfect"; the logo should be separated out, because with the emblem set between the words the name did not read clearly as "Gator Gold"; "fill up the screen a little bit"; "put something around it"; and it must "look good on a mobile app, or on a tablet", scaled correctly.
- The machine, sound, and play from features 1 to 5. Unchanged.

## Outputs

- A logo whose name reads "GATOR GOLD" as one unbroken line, with the emblem separate from it.
- Surroundings that fill the screen around the machine when there is room.
- The machine sized to the screen on phones, tablets, and laptops.

## Success criteria

1. The name reads "GATOR GOLD" in one unbroken line, with nothing between or over the letters.
2. The emblem is still on screen, apart from the name.
3. On a laptop and on a tablet, in either orientation, the space around the machine is filled with scenery rather than left blank.
4. On each screen size the machine is as large as fits, fully visible, and not stretched.
5. Nothing in the surroundings covers the machine or its controls.
6. On a phone held upright the machine fills the screen as before.
7. Play, sound, and payouts are unchanged.
8. With the device set to reduce motion, the surroundings hold still.

## Constraints

- No change to the machine's own layout, odds, payouts, or timing, apart from the logo.
- The surroundings are decoration. No new controls or information the player needs lives outside the machine.
- No images or files to download.
- A phone held sideways is out of scope for this feature.

## Evidence

- Screenshots at a small phone, a standard phone, a tall phone, a tablet upright, a tablet sideways, and a laptop.
- A measured check at each size that the machine is fully on screen, keeps its shape, and is not overlapped.
- A rerun of the play checks.
- Mark's reaction to how it looks.

## Stop

Stop when each criterion has evidence at every listed screen size.
