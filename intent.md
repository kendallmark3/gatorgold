Gator Gold

Progressive Intent v1

Intent

Create a polished, playful alligator-themed slot game for Stephanie.

The game should feel like a real animated slot machine, but it is a private entertainment game using virtual tokens only.

Stephanie starts with tokens, plays the slots, wins or loses tokens, and can eventually request that Mark personally redeem her winnings outside the game.

The first version should focus on making the core experience fun, attractive, responsive, and satisfying to play.



1. Inputs

Player starts with a virtual token balance.
Player chooses a wager.
Player presses SPIN.
The game generates the slot result.
Slot symbols include multiple alligator-themed characters and supporting swamp-themed symbols.
Suggested initial characters:

Happy Gator
Cool Gator
Queen Gator
Wild Gator
Baby Gator
Supporting symbols may include:

Gold coin
Diamond
Watermelon
Swamp flower
Treasure chest
The implementer may improve or simplify the symbol set.



2. Outputs

The running game should provide:

Animated slot reels
Clear token balance
Wager amount
Spin control
Visible winning combinations
Token winnings added automatically
Losing wagers deducted automatically
Celebration animation for wins
Larger celebration for unusually good wins
Alligator characters with personality
A Cash Out / Redeem option
Cash Out does not transfer money.

It should display something playful such as:

“See Mark to collect your winnings.”

The redemption request and token amount should remain visible so Mark and Stephanie can settle it personally.



3. Success Criteria

Version 1 succeeds when:

Stephanie can open the game and understand how to play without instructions.
She can repeatedly wager and spin.
Every spin produces a valid result.
Token accounting remains correct.
Winning combinations are obvious.
The reels animate smoothly.
The game feels colorful, polished, playful, and closer to a real slot experience than a software demo.
Alligator characters give the game a recognizable personality.
Winning feels rewarding through motion, sound, graphics, or combinations of those elements.
Stephanie can request redemption of her displayed token balance.
The game can be played for several minutes without errors or confusing states.


4. Constraints / Boundaries

Private entertainment game.
No advertising.
No purchases.
No payment processing.
No credit cards.
No external gambling platform.
No real-money transfer inside the application.
Tokens are virtual game credits.
Redemption, if Mark chooses to honor it, happens personally and outside the software.
Keep the first implementation small.
Do not over-engineer the architecture.
Do not add accounts, multiplayer, social features, tournaments, complex bonus systems, or unnecessary backend infrastructure in v1.
The implementer owns the technical solution.
Experience Boundary

The game should not merely function like a slot machine.

It should feel fun to spin.

Prioritize:

Spin → anticipation → reveal → reaction → reward → spin again.

That experience is the heart of the first build.



Evidence

Demonstrate the running game with:

Starting token balance
At least three different wager amounts
A losing spin
A normal winning spin
A larger winning spin
Correct token accounting after each
Alligator animation or character reaction
Successful Cash Out / Redeem request
After Stephanie plays the first working version, use her reaction and actual gameplay to determine Progressive Intent v2.

That last sentence is important. Don’t design v2 yet. Let Stephanie play it. If she says, “I want free spins,” “the gator needs to dance,” “I want jackpots,” or “this gets boring after five minutes,” that evidence becomes the next intent.

And I particularly like the redemption mechanic: “See Mark to collect your winnings.” You may have accidentally invented the world’s smallest casino. 😄
